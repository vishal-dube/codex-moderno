f. 2r · 2007 · Touch Bionics

Bionic hand

Della mano che obbedisce al pensieroOf the hand that obeys thought

The invention

The i-LIMB, released in Scotland in 2007, was the first commercially available prosthetic hand with individually powered fingers, driven by muscle signals read from the skin of the arm.

Music: Christian Petzold, Minuet in G major, BWV Anh. 114, performed by Aaron Dunn, piano (Musopen). CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.
