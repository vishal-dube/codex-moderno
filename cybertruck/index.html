Codice Modernof. 7r of 14

f. 7r · 2019 · Tesla

Cybertruck

Del carro di ferro sanza cavalliOf the iron cart without horses

The invention

Unveiled in November 2019 with a flat-panelled stainless steel body. At the launch, its 'armor glass' windows cracked when a metal ball was thrown at them. Deliveries began in 2023.

Music: Beethoven, Für Elise, performed by V. Gao, piano. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

The notes, translated

  1. questo carro si move per sé medesimo

    this cart moves by itself

  2. sanza cavalli e sanza vento

    without horses and without wind

  3. la forma sua è tutta di linee rette

    its form is all straight lines

  4. come uno triangolo posato in terra

    like a triangle laid on the ground

  5. a. b. c. il triangolo del corpo

    a. b. c. the triangle of the body

  6. il ferro non si piega

    the iron does not bend

  7. dinanzi. il lume è una sola linea

    from the front: the light is a single line

  8. le pietre non lo rompono ma il vetro sì

    stones do not break it, but the glass, yes

  9. g. la forza chiusa in vasetti

    g. the force shut up in little jars

  10. h. il motore che gira per virtù di calamita

    h. the motor that turns by virtue of the magnet

  11. i. e la ruota seguita il motore

    i. and the wheel follows the motor
