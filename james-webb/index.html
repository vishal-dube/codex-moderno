f. 11r · 2021 · NASA, ESA, CSA

James Webb Space Telescope

Dello specchio d'oro che guarda il principioOf the golden mirror that looks back to the beginning

The invention

Launched on 25 December 2021. Its 6.5-metre mirror is made of 18 gold-coated hexagonal segments, shaded from the Sun by a five-layer shield the size of a tennis court.

Music: Beethoven, 'Moonlight' Sonata, I. Adagio sostenuto, performed by Paul Pitman, piano (Musopen). Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

The notes, translated

  1. diciotto specchi d'oro fanno uno occhio

    eighteen golden mirrors make one eye

  2. che vede la luce partita

    that sees light which set out

  3. quando il mondo era giovane

    when the world was young

  4. d. lo specchio piccolo su tre gambe

    d. the small mirror on three legs

  5. a. cinque veli tra il sole e l'occhio

    a. five veils between the sun and the eye

  6. dalla parte del sole arde

    on the sun's side it burns

  7. dall'altra parte gela

    on the other side it freezes

  8. b. si piegò per entrare nel razzo

    b. it folded itself to fit inside the rocket

  9. c. e si aperse come uno fiore

    c. and opened like a flower
