f. 1r · 2007 · Apple

iPhone

Della tavoletta che parlaOf the little tablet that speaks

The invention

Announced in January 2007 as a phone, an iPod and an internet communicator in one device. Its multi-touch glass screen replaced the keyboard and set the shape of nearly every phone since.

Music: J. S. Bach, Prelude No. 1 in C major, BWV 846, performed by Kimiko Ishizaka, piano. CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.
