f. 14r · 2024 · SpaceX

Starship tower catch

Del gigante preso con le bacchetteOf the giant caught with chopsticks

The invention

On 13 October 2024 the returning Super Heavy booster, about 70 metres tall, was caught in mid-air by two mechanical arms on its launch tower, nicknamed the chopsticks.

Music: Beethoven, 'Ode to Joy' (Anthem of Europe), performed by United States Navy Band. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

The notes, translated

  1. questo vaso di fuoco alto come una torre

    this vessel of fire, as tall as a tower

  2. torna dal cielo

    comes back from the sky

  3. e la torre lo prende con due braccia

    and the tower takes it in two arms

  4. come le mani prendono uno uccello

    as hands catch a bird

  5. a. la torre di ferro

    a. the iron tower

  6. b. le braccia che si chiudono

    b. the arms that close

  7. c. le ali a graticola che lo guidano

    c. the gridiron wings that steer it

  8. d. trentatré fuochi di sotto

    d. thirty-three fires beneath

  9. e. torna voltandosi nell'aria

    e. it returns, turning in the air

  10. f. l'omo per misura

    f. the man, for measure
