f. 12r · 2024 · Neuralink

Neuralink implant

Del pensiero che move la frecciaOf the thought that moves the arrow

The invention

In January 2024 a coin-sized implant with 64 threads thinner than a hair was placed in a human brain by a surgical robot, letting a paralysed man move a computer cursor by thought.

    b. the threads in the flesh of the brain
