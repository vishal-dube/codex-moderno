f. 13r · 2024 · Apple

Vision Pro

Dell'occhio che vede due mondiOf the eye that sees two worlds

The invention

A headset released in February 2024 that puts 23 million pixels in front of the eyes and is controlled by where you look and small gestures of the hand.

Music: Greensleeves (anonymous, 16th century), performed by La Juglaresa / Emilio Villalba. CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

The notes, translated

  1. questo velo si mette dinanzi agli occhi

    this veil is set before the eyes

  2. e mostra uno mondo dentro al mondo

    and shows a world within the world

  3. guarda dove tu guardi

    it looks where you look

  4. e obbedisce al tocco delle dita

    and obeys the touch of the fingers

  5. a. gli occhi mostrati di fuori

    a. the eyes shown on the outside

  6. b. la vetrina della luce

    b. the little window of light

  7. c. la lente che piega i raggi

    c. the lens that bends the rays

  8. d. l'occhio come io lo disegnai

    d. the eye, as I drew it

  9. e. ventitré milioni di punti di luce

    e. twenty-three million points of light

  10. f. lumi che non si vedono guardano l'occhio

    f. unseen lights watch the eye
