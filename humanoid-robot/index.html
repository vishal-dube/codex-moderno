f. 5r · 2013 · Boston Dynamics

Humanoid robot

Dell'omo meccanicoOf the mechanical man

The invention

Atlas was unveiled in 2013 for the DARPA Robotics Challenge and later learned to run, jump and backflip; an all-electric version followed in 2024. Leonardo sketched a mechanical knight around 1495.

Music: J. S. Bach, Prelude No. 2 in C minor, BWV 847, performed by Kimiko Ishizaka, piano. CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.

The notes, translated

  1. come l'omo di Vitruvio

    like the man of Vitruvius

  2. così è misurato questo omo di ferro

    so this iron man is measured

  3. le braccia aperte quanto la sua altezza

    his open arms as wide as he is tall

  4. io feci uno cavaliere che si moveva

    I once made a knight that moved

  5. ora l'omo di ferro corre e salta

    now the iron man runs and leaps

  6. a. l'anello dentato

    a. the toothed ring

  7. b. la ruota molle che si piega

    b. the supple wheel that bends

  8. c. l'ovato che la spinge

    c. the oval that drives it

  9. d. salta e si volta nell'aria come il gatto

    d. it leaps and turns in the air like a cat
