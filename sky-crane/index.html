f. 4r · 2012 · NASA JPL

Mars sky crane

Del carro calato dal cieloOf the cart lowered from the sky

The invention

To land the nearly one-tonne Curiosity rover on Mars in August 2012, NASA lowered it on cables from a hovering, rocket-powered crane, which then flew away and crashed at a safe distance.

Music: Beethoven, Symphony No. 5, I. Allegro con brio, performed by Musopen. Public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

The notes, translated

  1. questo carro fu mandato in un altro mondo

    this cart was sent to another world

  2. e una gru di fuoco lo calò con funi

    and a crane of fire lowered it on ropes

  3. poi la gru volò via e cadde lontano

    then the crane flew away and fell far off

  4. a. discende con il carro in grembo

    a. it descends with the cart in its lap

  5. b. cala il carro con tre funi

    b. it lowers the cart on three ropes

  6. c. tagliate le funi fugge e muore

    c. the ropes cut, it flees and dies

  7. la polvere si volge come l'acqua

    the dust turns like water
